The line to use

Illustration by Carlos Peixoto — payforlayers.com (CC BY 4.0)

Put it wherever you credit your sources — a site footer, an about or colophon page, an image caption, a slide's notes, a README. It does not have to sit next to the illustration. If you can link, link payforlayers.com.

What this permits

  • Use in personal and commercial work, including work you sell.
  • Modification — recolouring, cropping, combining, redrawing.
  • Redistribution, including as part of something you charge for, provided the credit above travels with it.

What this does not permit

  • Dropping the credit.
  • Adding legal terms or technical measures that stop anyone else doing what this licence allows.
  • Suggesting that Carlos Peixoto endorses you or your work.

CC BY 4.0 is a permissive licence and it is irrevocable. It genuinely does allow someone to redistribute these PNGs, including for money, so long as they credit. That is a deliberate choice, not an oversight. If you are considering mirroring the set, please link here instead — it costs you nothing and it is the whole reason the free tier exists.

The paid set
