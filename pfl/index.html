Questions

Can I use these commercially?
Yes. Both sets are cleared for commercial work — client projects, products you sell, marketing. The only difference between them is credit and file format.
How do I credit them?
Put this anywhere the work is credited — a footer, an about page, a slide's notes:
Illustration by Carlos Peixoto — payforlayers.com (CC BY 4.0)
If crediting is awkward for where you're using them, the paid set removes the requirement.
What exactly is in the paid set?
The SVG of every illustration, a Figma file with each one as a component, and PNGs at the resolution they were drawn at. The SVGs are editable outlines — you can pull them apart, recolour them, or change the line weight.
Why is the free PNG smaller?
1080px on the long edge covers screens, which is where most of these end up. Above that you're doing production work — print, large format, or reworking the art — and that's what the paid set is for.
Can I redistribute them?
The free set is CC BY 4.0, which does permit redistribution as long as you credit. Please link back here rather than mirroring the files. The paid set is licensed to you and may not be redistributed or resold.
Do you take commissions?
Yes — carlos@carlospx.com. Custom sets in this style are the main reason this library exists.
What are they drawn with?
Brush pen on paper, scanned, then traced and cleaned up. The texture in the shading is from the original scan, not a filter.
